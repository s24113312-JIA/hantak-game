Tutorial

Step-by-step instructions. Click “Agree & Next”.

Welcome to Hantak FP Arcade — a first-person coin toss onto the circular stone.

Your twist: you get 3 attempts per level. Each win levels you up with a bigger prize. Lose all attempts and you must choose: Stop & Claim or Pass a Challenge Gate to continue.
Fairness: Heads/Tails faces are RNG (fair), while landing accuracy is influenced by your aim.

Mode

Single: one player climbs the ladder.
Multiplayer: 2 players take turns (hot-seat).

Names

Player 2 is used only in Multiplayer.
How to throw (skill):
1) Drag in the scene to aim toward the stone
2) Longer drag = more power (but too much becomes harder to control)
3) Release to toss
Rings: Bullseye (small) → Inner → Outer. Higher levels require better landings.
Attempts: Each level gives you 3 tries to win with your Heads/Tails call.

If you lose all attempts:
  • Stop & Claim: keep your secured prize
  • Continue: you must complete a Challenge Gate first
Leveling & prizes: Win a level → your reward tier increases.
Prizes can vary (example: plush toys, gadgets, vouchers). Your “secured prize” is what you keep if you stop.
Difficulty scaling: Each level adds stricter landing requirements and more scatter.
Free Trial: Before the real run, you’ll get a short trial (no prizes) to learn aiming.
After trial ends, the real ladder begins at Level 1 with 3 attempts.
